PWA for sharing location for seniors

A small location-sharing experiment.

Demo recording: PWA for sharing location for seniors

"Can you book an Uber for us?"

My parents would often be at Point A while I was at Point B, and the first step was always the same: getting their location to me.

WhatsApp made that harder than it needed to be. Finding location sharing wasn't obvious, and once they found it, the different options added more confusion.

So I built a small PWA that reduced it to one click.

They added their usual contacts once. After that, the PWA connected directly to WhatsApp and shared their location with a single tap.

It worked, but it was still a workaround.

Ideally, they shouldn't need the PWA, WhatsApp, Google Maps, or even Uber. An agent on their phone should understand the intent, get their location, find the available rides, and help complete the booking.

That's probably where I'll take this experiment next: seeing how much of that experience can actually be made agentic.

For now, the PWA was a small workaround for a very specific problem.

But it made me curious about what happens when we stop designing around apps and start designing around intent.
