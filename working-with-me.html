Working with me

How I work

I do my best work in ambiguity. I like turning messy problems into clear, tangible things we can act on.

Start with the problem

Question the brief. Challenge assumptions. Find the real problem

Bring me the messy version

I'd rather see rough thinking early than polished work too late

Make it tangible

Prototype, test, learn. I use AI and code to make ideas real before we overthink them

Challenge me

Disagree openly. Strong thinking matters more than being right

Think beyond the screen

Look for patterns, systems, and solutions that scale

As a leader

I give you context, not instructions

I expect designers to own the problem and find their path

Think, don't just execute

Know why you're making something, not just what you're making

Take ownership

See something that needs doing? Move it forward

Raise the bar

I'll push your thinking, craft, and impact. I expect you to push mine too

Own your growth

I'll create opportunities and give you honest feedback. You own what you do with it

Be curious. Be direct. Be low-ego

Care about the work, share your thinking, and make the people around you better
