Hi. I'm Priyanka

Ambiguity is where I do some of my best work. I enjoy finding clarity when the path forward isn't obvious, connecting the dots across teams and constraints, and turning that thinking into something people can actually use.

Over the last two years, AI has changed how I bring ideas to life. I use it to add logic and behavior to my designs, turning them into near-production-ready prototypes that can be tested and challenged early, saving engineering effort and organizational cost down the line.

Today, I lead two design teams across Commerce at Cimpress, partnering with product and engineering to shape strategy and define what we build across different business domains. I also work with a global team on design system consistency across products and teams.

Before Cimpress, I worked at Drona HQ, Freight Tiger, and Trepup.

The best work happens with curious people who take ownership, challenge assumptions, and enjoy figuring things out together.

Outside of work, I'm happiest when life slows down a little. A long walk in nature, a good game, music on a long drive, or an unplanned coffee with friends. Those simple moments are usually my favorite.
